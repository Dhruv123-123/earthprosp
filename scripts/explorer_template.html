What the colour means

With “Any commodity” selected, colour is the prospectivity score: the model’s ranking of how likely a hexagon is to host a deposit of any of the 15 classes. Choosing a commodity colours by that class’s share of the split, P(deposit) × P(class | deposit). Scales stretch to the 99th percentile of each view. Scores rank cells; they are not calibrated probabilities.

Where not to trust it

AlphaEarth sees the surface. Under glacial cover, rainforest, deep soil and young sediment the map is extrapolation. Training labels are densest in the US, part of the signal reflects visible mining disturbance, and the model has no geophysics yet.
