Mineral belts at 0.7 km²

The global EarthProsp map scores 36 km² hexagons. Here 15 of the world's major metallogenic belts and 4 under-explored frontier regions are re-mapped on H3 resolution-8 cells (~0.7 km², 34.0 million of them) from 160 m AlphaEarth pixels, and each fine model is tested against the coarse one on ground it never saw.

Capture AUC: share of held-out deposit cells captured, averaged over every area cutoff (0.5 = random). Five spatial block folds per belt with a 10 km dead zone. "Inside districts" ranks only the cells inside the 36 km² model's top 10% of ground: once a district is chosen, which square kilometre. Belts with too few deposit cells for five folds show the map only. The last four are frontier regions with few recorded deposits: they are mapped with one model trained on the other belts, and "pooled, unseen" is that model's score on them.
